About Amogh

School Captain Candidate 2026

I am not here to play politics. I am here because, just like you, I am exhausted by the status quo. The Fire & Ice campaign isn't just a catchy slogan; it's a completely new, unapologetic system of student governance.

Ice means I will listen to your deepest, unspoken frustrations with absolute empathy. I hear you. But listening is useless without action. That's where Fire comes in. I bring the strict discipline and relentless drive necessary to force real change.

Discipline isn't about restricting you—it's about holding the system and the rule-breakers accountable so that the rest of us can thrive in a fair, safe environment. You want more freedom, better events, and a better school life? That requires order and respect first. Choose someone who will fight for you, not just smile at you.

Campaign Promises

Inclusive Leadership

Every student matters. I will be the leader who listens without judgment, who welcomes all voices regardless of grade, background, or status. My door is open to everyone—always.

Approachable & Present

You won't find me hidden behind closed doors. I will be visible, accessible, and ready to listen. Whether in the hallway or at events, I'm the leader who actually cares about your day-to-day life.

Unity Without Bias

Division weakens us; unity defines us. I will bring every student together through real events, transparent decisions, and a student council that actually represents YOU. Under my leadership, no one will feel forgotten—because I lead for ALL, not just the privileged few.

Anonymous AI Justice

Bullies rely on your silence. We are stripping away their power. Our AI-assisted reporting system guarantees that if someone crosses the line, they will be dealt with swiftly, without anyone ever knowing it was you who spoke up.

Unrivaled Academic Excellence

Excellence is not a goal—it is my expectation. I will champion academic resources like no leader before me, securing better tutoring, smarter study materials, and opportunities that set our students up to dominate in every exam. Your success is my legacy.

Strict Discipline & Order

Rules exist for a reason. I will enforce clear, fair standards across all students—no exceptions, no favorites. Every student deserves a safe, orderly environment, and I will make sure that discipline is applied consistently and justly.
