About the Portal

SCIS Student Council 2026-27

The SCIS Student Portal is the official communication bridge between the student body and the Student Council of 2026-27. Our mission is to foster a community of transparency, accountability, and active participation.

We believe in the power of Student Voice. Whether it's through the anonymous feedback system, the idea box, or real-time progress tracking, we are committed to making your school experience the best it can be.

Guided by the values of South City International School, we strive for excellence in every initiative. This portal ensures that every student has a direct line to their representatives, and every promise made is a promise tracked.

Council Vision & Pillars

Inclusive Leadership

Every student matters. We will be the council that listens without judgment, welcoming all voices regardless of grade, background, or status. Our door is open to everyone—always.

Approachable & Present

We are not hidden behind closed doors. We are visible, accessible, and ready to listen. Whether in the hallway or at events, your council representatives are here for you.

Unity Without Bias

Division weakens us; unity defines us. We will bring every student together through real events, transparent decisions, and a council that actually represents YOU.

Student Wellbeing

Your safety and mental health are our priority. We are implementing better reporting systems and wellbeing initiatives to ensure a supportive environment for all.

Academic Support

Excellence is our goal. We will champion academic resources, peer-to-peer tutoring, and smarter study materials to help every student reach their full potential.

Integrity & Respect

We stand for values. We will promote a culture of mutual respect and integrity across all students—no exceptions. Every student deserves a safe and orderly environment.
